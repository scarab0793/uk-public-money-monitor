UK financial-year public finance data

ONS monthly data aggregated from April to March. Current central-government receipts and expenditure are different accounting measures from total public spending; do not treat their difference as the fiscal deficit. Incomplete years are flagged.

ONS annual public finance summaries

Calendar-year sums of central government current receipts and expenditure, and year-end public-sector debt as a share of GDP. Incomplete years are marked. These are different accounting measures and should not be subtracted to infer the fiscal deficit.
